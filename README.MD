Self-Sovereign Identity (SSI) it Develop on APTO / Move Network

SSI allows individuals to own and control their digital identity without relying on centralized authorities. Aptos Move enables:

Decentralized Identity Management: Users store their credentials on-chain, ensuring tamper-proof authentication.

Selective Disclosure: Users can share only necessary identity details, improving privacy and security.

Verifiable Credentials: Organizations can issue cryptographically signed credentials, preventing fraud
